# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: young men and families shopping contemporary Indian fashion, in Hyderabad, deciding what to try in store or enquire about on WhatsApp.

Secondary: the business owner, reviewing this as a digital storefront proposal.

## Product Purpose

A preview storefront for Mana Trendz Macha. It must prove brand, catalogue discovery, store visit, and WhatsApp enquiry — not checkout.

Success: the owner feels this is the online version of the brand, and a visitor can find a look and message the store.

## Positioning

Neighbourhood fashion floor with a premium digital presence. Conversion is WhatsApp and walk-in, not cart.

## Brand Commitments

- Official logo supplied by the client: black field, metallic gold MTM lockup, shopping-bag mark, crescent arc. Use this mark in the header, top left. Do not invent a replacement wordmark.
- Visual world requested by the client: gold and black. Remove the previous indigo / purple / yellow palette.
- No fake founder names, dates, awards, store counts, or live social URLs. Demo inventory, stores, and the WhatsApp number stay labelled as demonstration until confirmed.
- Checkout, UPI, and accounts are out of scope.

## Capabilities

Catalogue browse (men, kids, new arrivals, collections), product detail, wishlist (local), store list, about, contact preview form, WhatsApp enquiry, search.

## Constraints

- Next.js App Router, existing routes and data model stay.
- Photography is demonstration stock, not official campaign work.
- Accessibility: visible focus, reduced motion, keyboard paths.

## Evidence

Official logo raster at `public/brand/mtm-logo.png`. Product and store records in `/data` are labelled demo content.

## Voice

Young, confident, contemporary, accessible-premium. Plain verbs. No luxury-costume copy that the logo does not already own.
