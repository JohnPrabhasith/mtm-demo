# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: young men shopping contemporary Indian fashion across Telangana and Andhra, deciding what to try in store or enquire about on WhatsApp.

Secondary: the business owner, reviewing this as a digital storefront.

## Product Purpose

A storefront for Mana Trendz Macha. It must get a visitor to a real branch or a WhatsApp enquiry — not checkout.

Success: a visitor can find a look, pick a store, and message or call that floor.

## Positioning

Neighbourhood men's wear with a premium digital presence. Conversion is WhatsApp and walk-in, not cart.

## Brand Commitments

- Official logo supplied by the client: black field, metallic gold MTM lockup, shopping-bag mark, crescent arc. Use this mark in the header, top left. Do not invent a replacement wordmark.
- Visual world requested by the client: gold and black. Remove the previous indigo / purple / yellow palette.
- Official Instagram is confirmed: https://www.instagram.com/mana_trendz_macha/. Branch phones and Google Maps links on this site come from that bio. Do not invent other social URLs, founder names, dates, awards, hours, or street addresses that are not on those links.
- Checkout, UPI, accounts, shopping bag, and published rupee prices are out of scope until the client confirms live inventory.
- Do not list fake Hyderabad addresses (Banjara Hills, Secunderabad) or a demonstration WhatsApp number.

## Capabilities

Catalogue browse (men, looks, collections), product detail with WhatsApp enquiry, store list with real phones and maps, about, contact, search. Kids remains a secondary route, not primary navigation.

## Constraints

- Next.js App Router, existing routes and data model stay.
- Hero, categories, and lookbook use saved Instagram stills. Some catalogue SKUs still use stock photography — never show those as live prices.
- Accessibility: visible focus, reduced motion, keyboard paths.

## Evidence

Official logo raster at `public/brand/mtm-logo.png`. Official Instagram: `https://www.instagram.com/mana_trendz_macha/`. Lookbook stills in `public/instagram`. Confirmed branches in `data/stores.ts` (Wanaparthy, Kurnool, Nandyal, Kukatpally, Sangareddy, Nizamabad, Siddipet). Default WhatsApp is the Kukatpally bio number.

## Voice

Young, confident, contemporary, accessible-premium. Plain verbs. No luxury-costume copy that the logo does not already own.
